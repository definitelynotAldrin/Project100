Frequently asked questions

Do you attend after hours?

Yes, we operate 24/7 including weekends and public holidays. Emergencies don’t wait, and neither do we.

Are you Level 2 ASP accredited?

Yes, we are fully accredited Level 2 ASP (Accredited Service Provider) authorized to work on Ausgrid, Endeavour Energy, and Essential Energy networks.

How fast can you arrive?

Our typical arrival time is within 1 hour for Sydney metro areas. We prioritize safety-critical emergencies.

Do you issue NOSW/CCEW same day?

Yes, all required paperwork including NOSW (Notice of Service Work) and CCEW (Certificate of Compliance for Electrical Work) is lodged the same day.

Can you work with my insurer?

Absolutely. We provide detailed photos and comprehensive reports for insurance claims on the same day.
